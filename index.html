HELLO, I'M ANIKET

Curious about circuits.
Driven to build.

I'm a final-year VLSI Design and Technology student at APCOER in Pune, expecting to graduate in 2027. My work spans Verilog RTL, directed functional verification, and physical-design analysis using OpenLane/SKY130 and Synopsys tools.

My seven published Verilog projects range from ALUs, FIFOs, and fixed-point matrix multiplication to single-cycle and pipelined CPUs. The largest is a five-stage RV32IM + Zicsr microcontroller SoC with UART, GPIO, timer, SPI, and synchronous WFI sleep control.

At the 4th Annual SemiX Summit at IIT Bombay, I explored semiconductor entrepreneurship and joined a guided AI hardware workshop using systolic-array reference designs and the Synopsys backend flow.
